## The philosophy of art

### Definition
A branch of philosophy that asks conceptual questions about art, beauty, and the aesthetic experience. It is not a criticism, but the philosophical analysis of the concepts we use to talk about art.

### Two main branches
* Aesthetics: the study of sensory or sensori-emotional values, sometimes called judgments of sentiment and taste==(What is Beauty)==
* Philosophy of art: Focuses specifically on the nature of art and artworks==(What is Art?)==

### History Origins
- Ancient Greece
	- Plato: art as imitation/mimesis
	- Aristotle: art as representation and catharsis
- 18c
	- Alexander Baumgarten coins the term "aesthetics"(1750)
- 19-20c
	- The rise of <art for art's sake> and the shift from Beauty to Meaning.
